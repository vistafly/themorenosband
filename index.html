The Moreno's Band - Live Music Entertainment in Fresno and Central Valley California

The Moreno's Band is the Central Valley's premier family band, performing live music across Fresno, Visalia, Clovis, and beyond. We play Top 40, Oldies, Latin, Country, and more at bars, wineries, casinos, and private events. Book us for your wedding, party, or venue — check our upcoming gigs, watch live performances, read testimonials, and shop band merch.
